Search Tips

Finding the right images

Use specific keywords to narrow down your search. For example, instead of "dog", try "golden retriever puppy".

Copyright filters

Different copyright filters allow you to find images that you can use for various purposes:

  • Free to use: Images with Creative Commons licenses
  • Free for commercial use: Images that can be used in commercial projects
  • Free for noncommercial use: Images that can only be used in non-commercial projects
  • Free to modify: Images that can be modified or adapted

Transparent images

Checking "Transparent images only" will find images with transparent backgrounds, which are useful for design projects where you need to overlay images on different backgrounds.
